# Strays art style

The rules every pet, prop and accessory follows (spec §10.1). The aim is calm, friendly characters that read clearly at 72 pt on any wallpaper and never compete with the user's work.

## Shapes and lines

- **Flat vector, soft and round.** Ellipses and rounded paths; no sharp corners except ear tips, beaks and claws, and even those get a small join radius.
- **One outline weight:** 3 px on a 400 × 400 artboard, which is about 0.8 pt at Medium (104 pt) size. Round joins and caps. Thick strokes for limbs and tails are drawn as an outline stroke with a fur stroke 6 px narrower on top.
- **Silhouette first.** Each species must be recognisable as a filled black shape at 72 pt: cat by its round head, low wide ears and fluffy tail; corgi by its tall ears, long body and short legs; hamster by its round body and cheeks; budgie by its beak, crest-less round head and long tail.
- **Proportions:** chibi. The head is about 40% of the pet's height. Eyes are large, set low on the face.

## Colour

- **Coat:** base, one shade, one highlight, plus the outline, all bound to the `PetLook` view model (`coatBase`, `coatShade`, `coatHighlight`, `outline`). The app derives the other three from `coatBase` in OKLCH (spec §10.4).
- **Fixed palette for everything that isn't coat:**

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Eye iris | `#D9902E` (amber) |
| Pupil | `#2A1E18` |
| Eye glint | `#FFFFFF` |
| Nose, inner ear | `#E79A9A`, `#F0B4B0` |
| Blush | `#E77A7A` at 25% |
| Food bowl | `#5B8DEF`, food `#C98A4B` |
| Bed | `#B9A2E0`, cushion `#E9DFF8` |
| Rain cloud | `#9AA4B5`, drops `#6FA8DC` |
| Postcard | `#FFF6E0`, stamp `#E2725B` |
| Confetti | `#F2C14E`, `#E2725B`, `#5B8DEF`, `#7BC47F` |
| Zzz | `#8E8EA0` |

- **No pure black.** The darkest colour is the pupil.
- **Shading is flat:** never gradients. The shade sits on the underside and far side; the highlight on the chest or muzzle.

## Motion

- **Slow and soft.** Idle movements ease in and out (cubic 0.42/0.58). Nothing snaps except blinks.
- **Overlapping action:** tails and ears lag the body by 40–80 ms.
- **Moments are short:** blinks and twitches finish within 1 s, so Calm mode only has to play about a second at a time.
- **Big moves** (walk, celebrate, stretch) stay under 8 s and return to the seated pose.
- **Reduce Motion:** the app only plays blinks, so no clip may depend on a walk to make sense.

## Structure (every rig)

- 400 × 400 artboard, pet origin on the ground line at (200, 370), facing the viewer, seated.
- View model `PetLook`, with the four colours plus:
  - `mood` (number, 1 Joyful … 5 Gloomy), which picks the resting pose
  - `action` (number), which plays one-off clips; it returns to the pose afterwards

| `action` | Clip |
|---|---|
| 0 | none (rest in the mood pose) |
| 1 | eat (bowl fills, nibble, bowl empties) |
| 2 | petted |
| 3 | celebrate (species-specific, with confetti) |
| 4 | exit (walks out to the right) |
| 5 | enter (walks in from the right) |
| 6 | fall asleep (yawn, curl up, bed appears) |
| 7 | sleep (looping, with Zzz) |
| 8 | wake and stretch |
| 9 | enter carrying a postcard |
| 10 | walk (in place, for moving to a new spot) |

- Every clip starts and ends on the seated rest pose, so any clip can follow any other.
- Props live inside each rig's artboard, hidden (opacity 0) until a clip shows them.
- RML is generated by `art/<species>/make.py` (shared helpers in `art/tools/rml.py`). The generated `scene.rml` is committed and is what `rive` builds.
